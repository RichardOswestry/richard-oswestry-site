THE MERCY

THE LEDGER AND THE KEY — BOOK THREE

Some things are lost to time.
Some are hidden.

In a quiet house in Rishton, Sandra Kenny inherits more than a place to live.

A desk. A key. A ledger that was never meant to be read.

When a local locksmith opens what has remained sealed for centuries, they discover not treasure, but something far more enduring.

A life recorded. A family remembered. And a cipher that reveals just enough before withholding the one thing that matters most.

As the past begins to speak, Sandra discovers that some inheritances are measured not in gold, but in truth.
