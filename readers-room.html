THE READER'S ROOM

Ask Oswestry. He'll answer when he has time.

A place for readers to ask questions about the books, the stories, the ideas behind them, or anything else they would like to know.

Richard Oswestry reads the questions and, when time allows, chooses those he would like to answer.

Questions and answers are published here for everyone to read.

Not every question will receive an answer, but every genuine question will be read.

THE MORAL DILEMMA

Reader Question

Why did you decide to write a story that you knew some readers would find difficult?

Richard Oswestry

Some stories are comfortable to write. Others are not. This was a story I believed needed to be told, even knowing that some readers would disagree with it or find parts of it difficult.

Telling a story does not mean condoning everything that happens within it. Sometimes the purpose of a story is to make the reader confront a question rather than provide an easy answer.

Example only — this is how an approved question and answer will appear.
